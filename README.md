# 🤖 AI Lead Qualification System

Automated pipeline that captures, scores, routes, and CRM-tracks leads using AI — with zero manual work.

## 🎯 What It Does
- Captures leads from Tally.so form via webhook
- Filters spam and irrelevant inquiries at the first step with a  polite rejection email sent automatically, zero junk enters the pipeline!
- Scores each lead 1–10 using GPT-4o-mini (budget, urgency, company size, intent)
- Routes hot leads to priority Slack channel + fully personalized AI-written email
- Standard leads routed to separate channel with their own tailored reply
- Auto-creates HubSpot contacts with AI score, category, intent and reasoning attached
- Updates CRM status to Replied and logs timestamped timeline note when lead responds

## ⚡ Results
| Metric | Before | After |
|--------|--------|-------|
